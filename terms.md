## 1. What the Bot does

The Bot connects Discord servers to an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) run by the
operator. Depending on how the operator configures it, the Bot can:

- reply to direct messages from the operator and to messages in servers where it is
  set to listen (for example, when it is @mentioned or addressed by name);
- read message history, member lists, roles, channels, bans and audit logs in
  servers where management is enabled;
- create, edit and delete channels, roles and permission settings;
- delete, bulk-delete and pin messages;
- time out, kick, ban and unban members, change nicknames, and assign roles;
- run automations such as scheduled messages, welcome messages, automatic roles for
  new members, and keyword rules that reply to, react to, delete or report messages.
